6-month risk = 1 − Π(1 − p) over windows 1–6. Treating windows as independent overstates risk where quakes cluster, so the six-month figure errs on the cautious side at moderate and high risk (test: forecast 24.5% vs observed 22.4% on average; 0.379 bits of information gain). In the lowest decile the six-month forecast was 0.06% while 0.45% was observed, so treat values under 1% as "under 1%", not as zero. Grid, windows and model are provisional development outputs, not an official hazard product.
